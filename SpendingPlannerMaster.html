HOUSE FUNDING RULE

Spending on the house still counts as progress.

Upcoming payments are reserved first. When you mark one paid, Aurora reduces the House Pot cash and increases confirmed house spending by the same amount. The total funded progress therefore stays unchanged.

Funded progress = current House Pot cash + confirmed house spending. The payday allocator now uses this project progress—not the cash balance alone—to calculate the remaining gap.
